# Creator profiles

All 20 creators, built from their posts within the last 365 days. This is a description of each creator, not a ranking. Gates and scores come next.

Medians are used because one viral post can dominate. Missing values stay `n/a`: hidden likes, photo posts without play counts and unavailable reshares don't count as zero. Follower counts are shown but don't drive anything here.

**Label checks, read from captions and transcripts by Claude (no video, since the posts are on Instagram):** of 28 randomly chosen sponsorship labels, 26 look right and 2 can't be told from text. Every kids' food or nutrition brand below was read against its post, which added brands the earlier list missed, such as Good Monk. Child ages were checked wherever text states them, but many posts give no age, so these remain partly unconfirmed. The post `language` label isn't used, because it was wrong on 83% of the English-labelled posts checked.
